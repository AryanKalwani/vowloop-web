The loop

How VowLoop works

  1. 01

    Choose a goal and a partner

    Pick a goal you want help sticking with. Choose someone you trust to check your evidence.

  2. 02

    Show what you did

    Each time you do your goal, send the evidence you both agreed on.

  3. 03

    Your partner confirms your check-in

    Your partner reviews your evidence. Once they confirm it, your check-in is complete.

  4. 04 · Miss

    You pay your partner yourself

    Pay your partner outside the app, then tap to ask them to confirm.

The money

No payments move through VowLoop.
