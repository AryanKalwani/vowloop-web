Three steps. One loop.

Structured enough to be real. Simple enough to actually use every day.

01 · Agree

Write the vow with a partner

Pick a goal. Set the cadence and the stake. Choose someone you trust to hold the other side. Strict mode locks the rules; partner-confirm makes every check-in count.

02 · Check in

Show up, every period

Submit a short check-in on schedule. Add a proof note or photo if you opted in. Your partner confirms it — or, if you miss, the period closes and the stake posts to the ledger.

03 · Settle

Square up — privately

VowLoop tracks who owes what across the agreement. When you're ready, mark it settled. We don't move money. The trust and the transaction stay between you and your partner.

Built for promises that actually matter.

Streak apps reward consistency in a vacuum. VowLoop adds a person and a stake — the two things research and your own gut already know move behavior.

A · Stakes

Real consequences, your terms

You owe your partner directly — not an app, not a charity, not us. Money you'd actually hand them. Dinner you'd actually cook. A chore you'd actually take. VowLoop holds the ledger; the settlement happens between the two of you.

B · Grace

Built-in room for being human

Request grace when life genuinely happens. Your partner decides. Dispute a missed day if the system got it wrong. Firm, not punitive.

C · Privacy

Yours, not ours

Your agreements, photos, and ledger are visible only to you and your partner. Delete your account from inside the app at any time — your data goes with it.
