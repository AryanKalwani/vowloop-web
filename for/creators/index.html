How it works for creators.

Streaks alone don't ship. A partner with skin in the game does.

01 · Pick the output

One thing, daily-ish

500 words. A commit. A demo recording. One outreach DM. The smallest unit of progress you'd be embarrassed to skip three days in a row.

02 · Pick the partner

Someone who'll actually check

Your co-founder. Another indie hacker in the same season. Your spouse. Anyone who'll glance at the proof and call out a fake.

03 · Pick the stake

Big enough to feel

$10 straight to your partner per missed day. Or cover their dinner. Or take the gnarly chore for a week. Whatever you owe goes to them — not an app, not a charity. Big enough to be a real consequence, small enough you can afford to lose it.

Why this beats Notion + good intentions.

You don't have a discipline problem. You have a witness problem.

A · A real witness

Someone who notices

A streak that only you see doesn't break — it slowly stops mattering. A streak your partner reviews every day stays real.

B · Grace, not guilt

Built for the bad weeks

Sick. Trip. Family stuff. Request grace; your partner decides. The system stays firm, not cruel.

C · Private settlement

No payment rails, no fees

VowLoop tracks what you owe your partner. You settle directly with them — Venmo, cash, dinner, a chore. None of it touches the app and there are no fees.
