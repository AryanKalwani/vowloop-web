How it works for training.

Streaks die quietly. Stakes get noticed.

01 · Set the cadence

3×, 4×, 5× a week — your call

Daily run. Four lifts. Two cycling sessions. Whatever you've been promising yourself you'd do consistently and haven't quite managed.

02 · Pick a partner

Someone who trains too

Your gym friend. Your spouse. Anyone who'll glance at the proof and call out a faked check-in. Bonus: pair up mutually — they hold you, you hold them.

03 · Pick the stake

Money or chore — both work

$20 per missed session to their charity. Their leg day, your spotting. The dog walking for a week. Big enough to sting, small enough to lose.

Why this beats Strava streaks.

Strava counts the workouts you do. VowLoop notices the ones you don't.

A · Real friction for skipping

Skipping costs something

A red dot on Strava is easy to ignore. A $20 stake to your friend's charity is not. The consequence is the point.

B · Grace for life

Sick, injured, traveling

Request grace; your partner decides. The system doesn't shame you for being human, but you can't quietly skip every Friday and pretend.

C · Settled privately

No fees, no payment rails

VowLoop tracks who owes what. You settle however you settle — Venmo, beer, the next chore. None of it touches the app.
