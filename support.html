FAQ

Common questions

Who gets the money when I miss?

Your partner. You pay them yourself, outside the app, then tap to ask them to confirm.

No payments move through VowLoop. Your agreement and balance stay private between you and your partner.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the App Store, so you cancel in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then VowLoop.

Deleting your VowLoop account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings first.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. You'll be asked to confirm twice.

This permanently deletes your account, reminder settings, and your half of every agreement you've created. It cannot be undone.

Does my partner have to pay?

No. Supporting a partner is always free.
